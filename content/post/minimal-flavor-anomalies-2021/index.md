Together with David Marzocca, I proposed a minimal framework for the then-observed anomalies in charged- and neutral-current B decays, the muon magnetic moment, and the extraction of the Cabibbo angle. The model combines a scalar leptoquark with a charged singlet: the two mediators address complementary effects, and their one-loop interplay connects the full pattern of anomalies.

The paper, [*Minimal Explanation of Flavor Anomalies: B-Meson Decays, Muon Magnetic Moment, and the Cabibbo Angle*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.05730), was published in [*Physical Review Letters* 127 (2021) 061803](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.127.061803).
