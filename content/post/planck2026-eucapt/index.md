I co-organized [PLANCK2026 and the 6th EuCAPT Symposium](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1552644/), held jointly at CERN from May 18 to 22, 2026.

PLANCK2026 was the 28th meeting in the *From the Planck Scale to the Electroweak Scale* series, while the EuCAPT Symposium brought together the European theoretical astroparticle-physics and cosmology communities. The joint programme spanned formal theory, particle phenomenology, cosmology and astroparticle physics, with invited talks, contributed sessions and posters.

The [full programme and conference materials](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1552644/timetable/) remain available on CERN Indico.
