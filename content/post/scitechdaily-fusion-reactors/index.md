The [SciTechDaily article](https://scitechdaily.com/dark-matter-breakthrough-physicists-crack-big-bang-theory-puzzle/) describes how fusion reactors could produce axions and other exotic scalars, turning next-generation energy facilities into potential particle-physics laboratories. It is based on our paper, [Searching for exotic scalars at fusion reactors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12314).
