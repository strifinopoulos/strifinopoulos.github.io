title: 'Minimal Explanation of Flavor Anomalies: B-Meson Decays, Muon Magnetic Moment, and the Cabibbo Angle'
summary: >-
  We propose a minimal two-mediator framework that addresses the anomalies in charged- and neutral-current B decays, the muon magnetic moment, and the extraction of the Cabibbo angle. A scalar leptoquark and a charged singlet each address complementary effects, while their one-loop interplay allows the full pattern to be explained consistently with other phenomenological constraints.
